# GuardWork

Language for employer onboarding and Company participation. Company authority and Company approval are separate concepts.

## Language

**Employer Applicant**:
A registered person entering the employer journey who has not yet obtained a Company membership through invitation acceptance or successful new-Company dossier submission.
_Avoid_: Recruiter when no Recruiter membership exists, Company Owner before a Company is created

**Provisional Company Information**:
The Company name and work address declared during employer registration, before a legal dossier is submitted. This declaration establishes neither Company membership nor ownership.
_Avoid_: Registered Company, verified Company

**Company**:
The employer organization on whose behalf its authorized Company Members recruit.
_Avoid_: Account, Company Member

**Company Membership**:
A person's relationship with one Company for a membership period, carrying a Company role and membership status.
_Avoid_: Platform role, invitation

**Company Role**:
The authority category a person holds within a Company membership, such as OWNER, ADMIN or RECRUITER. It is separate from the person's platform role.
_Avoid_: Platform role, submitter job title

**Company Owner**:
A Company Member with the OWNER role; in new-Company onboarding, the applicant becomes Owner when the complete initial dossier is successfully submitted. Ownership does not mean the Company has been approved.
_Avoid_: Platform administrator, legal representative as a synonym for Owner

**Recruiter**:
A Company Member with the RECRUITER role, obtained in the onboarding journey through acceptance of a valid invitation after email verification.
_Avoid_: Employer Applicant, global Recruiter role

**Active Membership**:
A Company membership currently in ACTIVE status. Active membership alone does not establish email verification, Company approval or permission for every recruitment action.
_Avoid_: Verified Company, fully enabled account

**Company Invitation**:
An invitation addressed to one email recipient to join a particular Company as a Recruiter. An invitation is not a membership until valid acceptance.
_Avoid_: Company registration, membership

**Email Verification**:
Proof that a person controls the specific account email. It does not establish phone possession, Company membership or legal authority to represent a Company.
_Avoid_: Company verification, identity verification

**Declared Phone Number**:
The single normalized contact number shared by an account’s Candidate and employer contexts, subject to system-wide uniqueness. Its recorded presence does not prove possession by the account holder.
_Avoid_: Verified phone number without actual proof, legal identity

**Company Verification Dossier**:
The submitted Company information, legal document and representation declaration that Platform Staff reviews for Company approval.
_Avoid_: Registration form, membership request

**Company Verification**:
Platform Staff review of a Company's dossier resulting in VERIFIED or REJECTED. A Company awaiting a decision is PENDING; CHECKED describes completed processing and does not itself mean approval.
_Avoid_: Email verification, Owner activation

**Platform Staff**:
A platform actor authorized to perform assigned moderation and governance duties, including Company dossier decisions.
_Avoid_: Company Owner, Company administrator
